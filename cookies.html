Cookie Settings

Last updated: 13 October 2026

This website uses only essential storage and anonymous usage counts. We do not use advertising or tracking cookies.

1. Your Cookie Choice

On your first visit, we ask for your cookie preference. Your answer is saved in your own browser so that we do not ask again on every page. Choosing Decline switches off our anonymous usage counts. You can change your choice at any time.

Instead of cookies, this site uses your browser's local storage, which stays on your device and is never sent to SautiLume.

3. What We Do Not Use

  • Advertising or marketing cookies
  • Analytics that identify you, build a profile of you, or follow you across other websites
  • Social media tracking pixels
  • Any cookie or storage containing voice recordings, conversations, or biometric data

4. Anonymous Usage Counts

To improve SautiLume, our homepage and voice demo count what happens, such as a visit, the language chosen, which topic answered a question, or which helpline button was pressed, together with the type of browser and device. The counts never include your questions, our answers, your voice, your IP address, or any identifier, and they are stored with Google Firebase where only the SautiLume team can read them. They are switched off if you choose Decline, or if your browser sends a Do Not Track or Global Privacy Control signal. See our Privacy Policy for details.

5. Third-Party Links

Our website links to services such as LinkedIn, X (Twitter), and Google Maps. Those sites have their own cookie and privacy practices, which we do not control. We recommend reviewing their policies directly.

6. Managing Storage in Your Browser

You can view and delete everything this site has stored at any time through your browser's settings, usually under "Site settings" or "Cookies and site data". Clearing it will simply bring back the cookie notice on your next visit. For more on how SautiLume handles information, see our Privacy Policy.
