Cookie Settings

This website does not use tracking, advertising, or analytics cookies. There is nothing to opt out of.

What this site uses instead

sautilumeltd-prog.github.io does not set marketing or tracking cookies of any kind. We don't run ad networks, and we don't use analytics tools that track you across sites.

The live demo

The SautiLume voice demo uses your browser's local storage, not cookies, to remember one thing on your own device: your chosen language (English or Swahili), so you don't have to pick it again next time. If you optionally add a Hugging Face API token to try the AI-assisted answer mode, that token is also saved only in your browser's local storage on your device. None of this is ever sent to or stored by SautiLume's servers, and you can clear it any time by clearing your browser's site data or pressing Exit in the demo.

Third-party links

This site links out to services like LinkedIn and X (Twitter). Those sites have their own cookie and privacy practices, which we don't control. We recommend checking their policies directly.
