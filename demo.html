Karibu · Welcome

I am your private health and education assistant. Everything you share with me is confidential. Choose your language — Chagua lugha yako.

🔒 SautiLume does not store your name, your voice, or anything you say. Every conversation disappears when you leave. We only count, anonymously, how SautiLume is used (for example, how many questions were about menstrual periods) so we can make it better.

Emergency Help

I can connect you to someone who can help right now. Choose who to call. Calls to 999, 1195, 1199 and 116 are free of charge. All calls are confidential.

Settings

Without a token, SautiLume answers from its built-in verified health and education library. With a token, it uses the Gemma AI model. The token stays on this device only.
